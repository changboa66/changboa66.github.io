# Corner Puzzle Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 26, 2026**

Corner Puzzle ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Corner Puzzle iOS application.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect personal data.** Corner Puzzle works entirely offline and does not require an account. We do not operate a server or backend for the app.

## Photos

Corner Puzzle uses Apple's system photo picker. The app can access only the photo you explicitly select for a puzzle. Selected photos are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded or transmitted to us.

## Local Game Data

Puzzle results, best times, move counts, preview and undo counts, achievements, preferences, and daily challenge streaks are stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to us or shared with third parties. Deleting the app removes this locally stored data.

## Daily Challenges

Daily challenges are generated locally from the device's current date and bundled images. No network connection or remote data is used.

## Sharing

If you choose to share a result, the share card is generated locally. Photos selected by the user are strongly blurred by default and can be excluded from the card. Sharing occurs only after you invoke Apple's system share sheet and choose a destination. We do not receive or store shared content.

## Third-Party Services

Corner Puzzle does not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or third-party data collection services.

## Children's Privacy

Corner Puzzle does not knowingly collect information from children. Because the app collects no personal data, no children's personal information is transmitted to us.

## Your Rights

Because we do not collect or retain personal data, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct, sell, or delete. You can delete all locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

DuoCanvas Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 13, 2026

DuoCanvas ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the DuoCanvas iOS application.

     Information We Collect
     We do not collect personal data. DuoCanvas works entirely on your device and does not require an account. We
     do not operate a server or backend for the app.

     Photos
     DuoCanvas uses Apple's system photo picker. The app can access only the photos you explicitly select for
     editing. Selected photos are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded or transmitted to us.

     When you save a finished image, DuoCanvas asks for add-only permission to your photo library so it can write
     the exported file to Photos. The app cannot view or read other photos in your library.

     Local Editing Data
     Your edits, session files (originals, previews, and exports), and preferences are stored locally on your
     device. This information is not transmitted to us or shared with third parties. Deleting the app removes this
     locally stored data.

     Sharing
     If you choose to share a result, sharing occurs only after you invoke Apple's system share sheet and choose a
     destination. We do not receive or store shared content.

     Purchases
     If you make an in-app purchase, payment is processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store
     your payment information.

     Third-Party Services
     DuoCanvas does not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or third-party data collection services.

     Children's Privacy
     DuoCanvas does not knowingly collect information from children. Because the app collects no personal data, no
     children's personal information is transmitted to us.

     Your Rights
     Because we do not collect or retain personal data, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct,
     sell, or delete. You can delete all locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device.

     Changes to This Policy
     We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated
     revision date.

DuoCanvas Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 11, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how [DEVELOPER OR LEGAL ENTITY NAME], the developer of DuoCanvas (“we,” “our,” or “us”), handles information when you use the DuoCanvas iOS application and its official website.
Photos, Videos and Editing Content
DuoCanvas processes your photos, videos, screenshots and entered text locally on your device. We do not upload this content or your editing projects to our servers or to our analytics provider.
DuoCanvas does not require an account. It uses Apple’s system media picker, or content you explicitly share into the app, to receive files you choose for editing.
When you save an export, DuoCanvas requests add-only permission to write the finished image or video to Photos. This permission does not give the app general access to browse your photo library.
Local Projects and Preferences
Project files, selected media copies, previews, exports and preferences are stored locally inside the app.
Deleting a project removes its associated local project files. Uninstalling DuoCanvas removes its local app data. Files you have already saved to Photos, Files or another destination are separate and are not removed when you uninstall the app. Copies in device backups are managed through Apple’s backup services and your settings.
Optional Usage Analytics
Usage analytics is disabled unless you choose to enable it. DuoCanvas asks whether you would like to share usage statistics to help improve the app.
Declining does not restrict any editing features. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Usage analytics.
When enabled, DuoCanvas sends limited usage information to PostHog, our analytics service provider, using its US Cloud service. This information may include:
• Features and screens opened, and selected layout identifiers.
• Time spent using features while the app is in the foreground.
• Item counts and import, export, save and sharing-action outcomes.
• Screenshot-overlap analysis counts.
• Fixed error categories, such as permission or storage errors.
• App version, build number, platform and event timestamps.
• A randomly generated installation identifier and session identifiers.
The installation identifier links events from the same app installation. It is not your name, email address, Apple account or advertising identifier. However, these statistics are pseudonymous rather than completely anonymous.
We do not send photos, videos, screenshots, entered text, project titles, filenames, file paths or project contents as analytics data. We do not use session replay or record your screen.
Location enrichment is disabled. Network connections nevertheless expose an IP address and other technical request information to the service provider.
For information about PostHog’s privacy practices, see PostHog’s Privacy Policy.
Disabling Analytics
Turning usage analytics off stops new analytics collection, cancels pending uploads, clears locally queued events and removes the local analytics installation identifier.
If you enable analytics again, the app creates a new installation identifier.
Disabling analytics does not automatically erase information already received by PostHog. Requests already received by the service cannot be recalled through the switch. You may contact us about previously transmitted data.
Data Retention
Local editing projects remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app, subject to your device’s backup settings.
While analytics is enabled, unsent events may be queued locally. Events older than seven days are discarded when the queue is processed. The queue holds no more than 500 events and is cleared when analytics is disabled.
Analytics data already transmitted to PostHog is retained according to [CONFIRMED CLOUD RETENTION AND DELETION PRACTICES].
Support correspondence is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and handle related support matters.
Sharing
Sharing occurs only when you invoke the system share sheet and select a destination. We do not receive your shared content through this process.
The selected destination may process or upload your content according to its own privacy practices.
Advertising and Tracking
DuoCanvas does not include advertising or use the advertising identifier.
We do not use analytics for targeted advertising, cross-app advertising tracking or selling data to data brokers.
Support and Website Visits
If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide so we can respond. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive content.
Our official website is hosted on Cloudflare. Website requests pass through Cloudflare, which may process IP addresses and technical request information for delivery and security. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.
Your Choices and Rights
You can decline or disable usage analytics, delete local projects and control Photos permissions through your device settings.
Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal data, withdraw consent, or complain to a relevant data protection authority.
Contact us to make a request. We may need additional information to identify the relevant records, and we may not be able to associate pseudonymous analytics events with a named individual.
Children’s Privacy
DuoCanvas is a general-purpose creative tool and does not ask children to provide names, contact details or account information.
If you believe a child has provided personal information that requires deletion, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action under applicable law.
Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy when our features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent.
